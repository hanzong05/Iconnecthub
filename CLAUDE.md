## How to work on this project

For any big change (new feature, new data, anything touching sign-in, roles, hubs, publishing, the guest app or stored data), follow `.claude/skills/engineering-workflow.md` from step 1 to the checks at the end, and load the skills it names for the task: react-nextjs-senior-engineer, application-security, backend-database-engineer, ui-ux-design-engineer, qa-testing-engineer, legacy-integration-engineer.

Before finishing a big change, report by skill: what was checked, commands run with real results, what was not run, and the risks left. Small fixes (a typo, a colour) can skip the full workflow.
